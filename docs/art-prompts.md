# Extra art prompts

Optional artwork beyond the monster sheets (see `sprite-prompts.md`). None of it is required: the game draws all of this itself until an image is supplied. Listed in order of how much each one improves the game.

| Art | Images | Save as |
|---|---|---|
| Battle backdrops | 7 | `bg-meadow.png`, `bg-mountain.png`, `bg-volcano.png`, `bg-darkwood.png`, `bg-snow.png`, `bg-coast.png`, `bg-hall.png` |
| Characters | 1 sheet of 16 | `characters.png` |
| Title screen | 1 | `title.png` |
| Sigils and items | 1 sheet of 16 | `icons.png` |

## Tips

- Attach your first monster sheet as a style reference for everything here, so the whole game looks like one artist drew it.
- Backdrops and the title screen are 3:2 landscape. The two sheets are square, like the monster sheets.
- Check the two sheets against their cell lists before keeping them: 16 distinct things, in the right order, not touching each other.
- As with the monsters, the prompts describe the style in words and never name another game.

## 1. Battle backdrops

One prompt per scene. They share everything except the last paragraph. The game places the monsters, their shadows and the message box on top, which is why the prompts ask for clear patches of ground and an empty bottom quarter.

### Meadow (`bg-meadow.png`)

Used for: Routes and towns in the south.

```text
A battle background for a monster-taming RPG. Landscape format, 3:2 (for example 1536 x 1024).

STYLE: Pixel art in the style of a classic handheld monster-collecting RPG: crisp square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no 3D render, no painterly texture. No smooth gradients: use stepped bands of colour and a little dithering instead. A limited palette of about 24 colours. Light comes from the top-left; shadows shift toward blue-violet and lit areas toward warm yellow.

COMPOSITION: seen from a low three-quarter angle, as if standing on the field. The horizon sits about 45% of the way down from the top. Above it is sky and distant scenery. Below it is open, mostly flat ground filling the lower half of the image. Keep two patches of ground clear and uncluttered, because creatures will be placed on them later: one on the right just below the horizon, and a larger one at the lower left. Put nothing important in the bottom quarter; a text box will cover it.

EMPTY SCENE: no creatures, no people, no platforms or circles marked on the ground, no text, no interface, no borders, no watermark.

SCENE: A bright summer meadow at midday. Rolling green hills in the distance with a line of round leafy trees, a pale blue sky with a few small white clouds, lush grass in the foreground with a scatter of wildflowers at the edges.
```

## 2. Characters (`characters.png`)

The 16 named people in the game: you, your rival, Sage Maren, the eight wardens, the four councillors and the High Warden. Each full-body figure is used when that person steps onto the field before a battle, and the head and shoulders are cropped for their portrait in dialogue.

```text
A 4x4 sprite sheet: a square image divided into a strict grid of 16 equal square cells, 4 columns by 4 rows, with exactly one character in each cell and nothing else. Cells are listed left to right, top to bottom.

STYLE: hand-pixelled 2D trainer sprites for a classic handheld monster-collecting RPG. True pixel art: crisp square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no 3D render. Each character uses a small palette of about 16 colours with 3 or 4 shades per colour. Light comes from the top-left; shadows shift toward blue-violet and highlights toward warm yellow. Outlines are one pixel wide in a dark tone of the nearby colour, not pure black. Friendly, slightly stylised proportions, about five heads tall.

VIEW: every character is shown full body from head to toe, standing in a relaxed, characterful pose, in a front three-quarter view turned slightly to the left, with the face clearly visible. All 16 are drawn at the same scale and fill about 85% of the cell height, centred, with clear space around them and plenty of space between neighbours.

BACKGROUND: one flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) across the whole image. No ground, no cast shadows, no scenery, no frames, no grid lines, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermark.

DESIGNS: all 16 are original characters; do not copy or resemble any existing franchise character. Never write the names in the image. Cells 4 to 11 are town wardens, adults who each wear a small gold diamond-shaped badge on the chest. Cells 12 to 15 are the council, older and more formal. No creatures or pets in any cell.

THE 16 CELLS:
1. (row 1, column 1) The hero: a young, androgynous adventurer with short dark brown hair and a gold headband, a coral-red jacket, a gold scarf, navy trousers and a small satchel. Bright and determined, one hand on the satchel strap.
2. (row 1, column 2) Corvin, the rival: the same age as the hero, with spiky silver hair, a dark indigo jacket, a lavender scarf and dark trousers. Arms crossed, a confident smirk.
3. (row 1, column 3) Sage Maren: an elderly woman scholar with white hair in a bun, small round spectacles, a cream robe with teal trim and a leather-bound journal under one arm. Warm and kindly.
4. (row 1, column 4) Warden Fern, plant warden of an ivy-covered town: a patient gardener in moss green and earth brown, with a straw hat, a canvas apron, rolled sleeves and a sprig of ivy at the shoulder. Gentle smile.
5. (row 2, column 1) Warden Marlow, water warden of a harbour town: a weathered harbour master in a long sea-blue coat with brass buttons, a knitted cap and tall boots. Stern, arms behind the back.
6. (row 2, column 2) Warden Volta, electric warden of an engineering town: an energetic engineer in a yellow and charcoal work jacket, goggles pushed up on the forehead, a tool belt and thick gloves. Grinning, one fist on the hip.
7. (row 2, column 3) Warden Garnet, rock warden of a town carved from stone: a broad, solid stonemason in grey-brown with a deep garnet-red sash, heavy boots and dusty forearms. Unhurried and stoic, arms folded.
8. (row 2, column 4) Warden Ignacia, fire warden of a volcano town: a confident smith in coral red and orange with a dark leather apron, a bandana, and a smudge of soot on one cheek. Chin up, hand on hip.
9. (row 3, column 1) Warden Aerin, wind warden of a hilltop town: a light, airy figure in pale blue and white with a long scarf streaming to one side and a feather tucked in the hair. Calm, eyes half-closed in the breeze.
10. (row 3, column 2) Warden Morwen, shadow warden of a town of lamps: a quiet lamplighter in a dark violet hooded cloak, holding a small lit lantern low at one side. Composed and mysterious.
11. (row 3, column 3) Warden Halvard, ice warden of the last northern town: a tall, grey-bearded man in a heavy fur-lined coat of pale blue and white, with thick mittens. Stern and steady.
12. (row 3, column 4) Councillor Thane, master of metal and stone: a heavy-set figure in iron-grey plate over a dark crimson hooded cloak. Immovable, both hands resting on the belt.
13. (row 4, column 1) Councillor Sybil, the seer: a slender figure in layered violet hooded robes with a crystal pendant, a knowing half-smile and one hand raised slightly.
14. (row 4, column 2) Councillor Rook, master of shadow: a lean figure in a near-black indigo hooded cloak, the upper face half in shadow. Silent and still.
15. (row 4, column 3) Councillor Brine, the old angler: a jovial, round older man in a wide-brimmed hat with a flower tucked in the band, a sea-blue coat and a fishing creel at the hip. Laughing.
16. (row 4, column 4) High Warden Isolde, champion of the region: a regal woman with long golden hair, a white and gold formal coat, a gold cape over one shoulder and a small crown-shaped brooch. Calm and assured.
```

## 3. Title screen (`title.png`)

The game draws the wordmark, the menu buttons and a parade of monsters on top of this, so the image itself must contain no text.

```text
The title screen background for a monster-taming RPG set in a region called Vessara. Landscape format, 3:2 (for example 1536 x 1024).

STYLE: Pixel art in the style of a classic handheld monster-collecting RPG: crisp square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no 3D render, no painterly texture. No smooth gradients: the sky is built from stepped bands of colour with a little dithering. A limited palette built around deep midnight navy (#171629), dark indigo (#25213D), soft lavender (#A89ABF), coral (#DB665A) and muted gold (#D9AD55).

SCENE: a wide landscape at dusk. A night-blue sky at the top fading through violet to a coral and gold glow at the horizon, scattered stars, a pale full moon low on the right, a few thin clouds catching the last light. Three layers of mountain ridges in darkening indigo, the farthest with pale snow caps. In the middle distance a small town with a handful of warm lit windows. In the foreground a dark grassy hilltop with pine silhouettes at the left and right edges.

COMPOSITION: the top 40% is calm open sky, because a logo will be placed there. The centre is uncluttered, because menu buttons will sit there. The bottom 20% is a dark, flat strip of ground along which creatures will walk.

EMPTY SCENE: no creatures, no people, no title, no logo, no text, no interface, no borders, no watermark.
```

## 4. Sigils and items (`icons.png`)

The eight sigils you earn from the wardens, and eight of the twelve bag items. Grand Elixir, Ward Incense, the Fishing Rod and the Town Map keep their current icons.

```text
A 4x4 sheet of 16 game item icons: a square image divided into a strict grid of 16 equal square cells, 4 columns by 4 rows, with exactly one object in each cell and nothing else. Cells are listed left to right, top to bottom.

STYLE: hand-pixelled inventory icons for a classic handheld monster-collecting RPG. True pixel art: crisp square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no 3D render. Bold, simple shapes that stay readable when shrunk very small. Each icon uses about 5 to 8 colours. Light comes from the top-left; shadows shift toward blue-violet and highlights toward warm yellow. A one-pixel outline in deep midnight navy (#171629).

VIEW: each object is seen straight on, centred, filling about 70% of its cell, all at a consistent size, with clear space between neighbours.

BACKGROUND: one flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) across the whole image. No ground, no cast shadows, no frames, no grid lines, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermark.

DESIGNS: all original; do not copy any existing franchise item. Cells 1 to 8 are a matching set of badges called sigils: each is the same diamond shape (a square standing on one corner) with the same muted gold (#D9AD55) rim, and differs only in its enamel colour and the emblem in the centre. The charms in cells 9 to 11 are flat diamond-shaped amulets hanging from a cord loop; they must not look like balls or capsules.

THE 16 CELLS:
1. (row 1, column 1) Sprout Sigil: leaf-green enamel with a two-leaf sprout emblem.
2. (row 1, column 2) Current Sigil: sea-blue enamel with a curling wave emblem.
3. (row 1, column 3) Circuit Sigil: bright yellow enamel with a lightning bolt emblem.
4. (row 1, column 4) Bedrock Sigil: grey-brown enamel with an emblem of stacked stone layers.
5. (row 2, column 1) Cinder Sigil: coral-red enamel with a flame emblem.
6. (row 2, column 2) Zephyr Sigil: pale sky-blue enamel with a swirl of wind emblem.
7. (row 2, column 3) Dusk Sigil: deep violet enamel with a crescent moon emblem.
8. (row 2, column 4) Rime Sigil: icy blue-white enamel with a six-pointed snow crystal emblem.
9. (row 3, column 1) Tin Charm: a plain dull grey tin amulet on a brown cord loop.
10. (row 3, column 2) Silver Charm: a polished silver amulet with a small blue gem, on a cord loop.
11. (row 3, column 3) Gold Charm: a gleaming gold amulet with a small red gem and a tiny sparkle, on a cord loop.
12. (row 3, column 4) Tonic: a small round glass bottle of red liquid with a cork stopper.
13. (row 4, column 1) Elixir: a taller, slimmer glass bottle of amber liquid with a cork stopper.
14. (row 4, column 2) Ambrosia: an ornate golden flask with a soft glow and a small wing-shaped handle.
15. (row 4, column 3) Panacea: a small bundle of green healing herbs tied with pale string.
16. (row 4, column 4) Wakeroot: a knobbly brown root with two fresh green leaves sprouting from the top.
```
